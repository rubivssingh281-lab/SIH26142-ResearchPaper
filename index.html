Natural Language Processing in the Patent Domain: A Survey

A comprehensive survey equipping NLP researchers with essential knowledge to navigate patent analysis and generation. Covers nine patent analysis tasks and four generation tasks, mapping how LLMs can be leveraged for this complex legal-technical domain.

02 ScienceDirect · Intelligent Systems · 2024

Tree Boosting Methods for Balanced and Imbalanced Classification and Their Robustness Over Time in Risk Assessment

Explores tree-based boosting techniques for handling class imbalance in classification tasks — directly relevant to patent grant prediction and prior-art risk scoring where positive and negative samples are heavily skewed.

03 ACL 2024 · Long Paper

Grounding Language Model with Chunking-Free In-Context Retrieval

Introduces the CFIC retrieval approach for RAG systems — eliminating document chunking by using encoded hidden states for in-context retrieval. Incorporates Constrained Sentence Prefix Decoding and Skip Decoding for precise evidence grounding in long patent documents.

Qian et al. ACL 2024, Bangkok Read Paper
04 ACL 2024 · Long Paper

Generate-then-Ground in Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Multi-hop Question Answering

Proposes the GenGround framework that synergizes LLM parametric knowledge with external documents for multi-hop QA — decomposing complex patent queries into simpler single-hop questions, generating answers, then grounding them in retrieved documents.

Shi et al. ACL 2024, Bangkok Read Paper
05 TIPR · IP Research · 2024

Traditional Knowledge Digital Library as Patent System Facilitator

Examines India's TKDL and its role in preventing biopiracy by serving as prior art against illegitimate patent claims on traditional knowledge. Proposes a TKDL fund model for indigenous community development through controlled third-party access.
